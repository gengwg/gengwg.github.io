Hello

I'm a Site Reliability Engineer in the San Francisco Bay Area, working at the intersection of AI infrastructure and reliability. I build and operate large-scale GPU clusters — Kubernetes, Slurm, observability, the whole stack — for companies running LLM inference and training.

Before this, I spent a decade in experimental particle physics: searching for the Higgs boson at Fermilab, measuring CP violation in single top quarks, and reconstructing PET images of the brain. I earned my PhD at Michigan State and spent time at CPPM in Marseille on an Eiffel Scholarship. I still think like a physicist — root-cause analysis, first-principles reasoning, and a low tolerance for things that "just work by accident."

I've been lucky to work on some big things: 3,000-GPU Kubernetes clusters at Meta, 10,000+ physical and virtual machines provisioned at Machine Zone, monitoring systems covering 300+ services at Walmart, and most recently an 800-node GPU fleet at Aranya. I care about making systems observable, automating away toil, and building on-call practices that don't burn people out.
